Chiang Mai 2025

Background

  • Who: CAPTSLAM Townsquare
  • What: 8 days trip with the entire committee
  • When: Jan 4-11 2025
  • Where: Chiang Mai, Thailand
  • Why: Volunteer at a local primary school + R&R

Day 1: Flight + Night market

We gave each other "Ugly T-shirts" as secret santa gifts. Mine was a shirt made entirely of corn, and we wore them the entirety of the flight, changing into them at T4.

After looking like fools on 2 short flights, we arrived and got settled at the Core Gallery Hotel, then went to the night market to try some street food and do some shopping.

Day 2: Artist Village + Mon Jam

Personally I could care less for crafts and stuff, but the village itself was nice. We made fridge magnets in a workshop, then wandered around more to look at all the small shops. Khao Soi for lunch.


After that we went to Mon Jam, a mountain with a nice view of the valley. There were many flowers.

We fed some sheep and took many pictures. All in all, a nice break from the fast-paced city life.

Day 3: CMU

We had lunch at this amazing restaurant with a small lake inside called "Huen Suthep". The food was amazing, and we took some nice pics too.


We had a half-day tour of Chiang Mai University, which was very beautiful and had a lot of greenery.

We first had a welcome briefing with "the myth the legend" and the literature students who were our guides for the day. We then went to see the famous bridge over the Ang Kaew Reservoir, which is located in the middle of the campus.

Legend has it that if you run across the bridge in one breath, your wish will come true. Of course I made it across, but I'm still waiting on that wish :D

Another night market run after CMU.

Day 4: School Day 1

We had our first day of volunteering at the local primary school. It was a great experience interacting with the students and helping them with their English lessons.

The students were very lively and fun to be around, and even with the language barrier, we managed with just Google Translate and some hand gestures. We played Ice and Water, Virus, and many other games with them.

Luckily the guys bought matching bucket hats at the night market, the sun was a deadly lazer.

Dinner at some restaurant where I said "fanum tax" to Winnie and she said "how old already" in Chinese with a most disturbing side eye.

Then I hit her with 5 big booms.

Day 5: School Day 2

Our second day at the school was even more entertaining, with games like building the tallest tower made of straws and cups, as well as a parachute out of paper to float an egg down from the 2nd floor.

Finally being in engineering was more than just a smelly major.


We played with more kids during recess and counted to 10 many times in Thai (but mostly to 4 since that was my group number).

Neung Song Sam Si(gma).

After school we went to the cat cafe near our hotel that we had been eyeing for days prior. All of us turned into Disney princesses and locked in immediately.

We had this mookata place for dinner but everything went wrong.

Firstly my table got 2 siaolangs who brought back 2 plates of sausages and enoki mushrooms, then the stove didn't get up to temperature and the food wasn't cooking well.

Jong and I were just appalled by "sausage girl" and "mushroom girl" and banned sausages from future dinners.

Then we went to the hidden gem "Massage Near Me" and got backshots, and the shopkeeper had to call in reinforcements to serve all 9 of us. Then aunties started appearing on their bikes one by one like in Endgame.

Supper was grabfood Taco Bell, Jong and I tried to tell the funniest story ever but these people just don't get humour.

Day 6: School Day 3

Last day with the students, we played more games and performed APT for them after a few days of practice. I never wanted to hear that song ever again after that day.

After a sad farewell, we went to aura farm at this crafts market thingy, but the whole place was kinda closed. So we just took pics then went for the dinner reservation.

Dinner was extremely hyped up but was kinda just alright. I don't even have any pics of the food. Here are the tomato guys instead.
