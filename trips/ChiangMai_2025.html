Chiang Mai 2025

Background

  • Who: CAPTSLAM Townsquare
  • What: 8 days trip with the entire committee
  • When: Jan 4-11 2025
  • Where: Chiang Mai, Thailand
  • Why: Volunteer at a local primary school + R&R

Day 1: Flight + Night market

We gave each other "Ugly T-shirts" as secret santa gifts. Mine was a shirt made entirely of corn, and we wore them the entirety of the flight, changing into them at T4.

After looking like fools on 2 short flights, we arrived and got settled at the Core Gallery Hotel, then went to the night market to try some street food and do some shopping.

Day 2: Artist Village + Mon Jam

Personally I could care less for crafts and stuff, but the village itself was nice. We made fridge magnets in a workshop, then wandered around more to look at all the small shops.

After that we went to Mon Jam, a mountain with a nice view of the valley. There were many flowers.

We fed some sheep and took many pictures. All in all, a nice break from the fast-paced city life.

Day 3: CMU

We had lunch at this amazing restaurant with a small lake inside called "Huen Suthep". The food was amazing, and we took some nice pics too.

We had a half-day tour of Chiang Mai University, which was very beautiful and had a lot of greenery.

We first had a welcome briefing with "the myth the legend" and the literature students who were our guides for the day. We then went to see the famous bridge over the Ang Kaew Reservoir, which is located in the middle of the campus.

Legend has it that if you run across the bridge in one breath, your wish will come true. Of course I made it across, but I'm still waiting on that wish :D
